The one idea to hold onto

A control system keeps a variable near its set point by pushing against every drift.

Common mistake

Students say the response stops the drift at once. It slows the drift, and then the variable returns.

Phenomenon

A runner finishes a race in hot air. Her core temperature rises by less than one degree.

Written response3 marks

Explain how sweating helps return core temperature to its set point after a hard run.

Scoring guide
  • You say the sensor notices a rise in core temperature.
  • You say the control center signals the sweat glands.
  • You say evaporating sweat takes heat from the skin.

Model answer

In your own words, say why the response to a drift has to point the opposite way.

One way to say it

A response that pushed the same way would make the drift bigger. A response that pushes the opposite way brings the variable back.

Why the cooling slows as the gap closes

Extra depth. The rest of the lesson works without it.

The rate of heat loss is proportional to the gap between the cup and the room. Halve the gap and the rate halves. A curve like this is called an exponential approach.

Set points can move

During a fever the set point itself rises. The body then works to hold the higher value, which is why a fever can bring chills.

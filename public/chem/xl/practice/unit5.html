Heating & Phase Change Equations
Specific Heat Formulaq = m × c × ΔT (where ΔT = T_final - T_initial)
Heat of Fusion (Melting / Freezing)q = m × H_f (or q = n × ΔH_fus)
Heat of Vaporization (Boiling / Condensing)q = m × H_v (or q = n × ΔH_vap)
Calorimetry Heat Exchangeq_reaction = -q_solution = -(m_solution × c_solution × ΔT)

Reaction Enthalpy from BondsΔH_rxn = Σ Bond Energies (Bonds Broken) - Σ Bond Energies (Bonds Formed)
Hess's Law & Enthalpy of Formation
Standard Enthalpy of Formation (ΔH°_f)ΔH°_rxn = Σ n ΔH°_f(products) - Σ m ΔH°_f(reactants)
Elements in Standard StateΔH°_f = 0 kJ/mol (e.g. O₂(g), N₂(g), C(graphite), Fe(s))
Sign ConventionΔH < 0 is Exothermic (heat released); ΔH > 0 is Endothermic (heat absorbed)

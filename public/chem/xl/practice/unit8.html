Empirical & Combined Gas Laws
Boyle's Law (constant T, n)P₁ × V₁ = P₂ × V₂ (Inverse relationship)
Charles's Law (constant P, n)V₁ / T₁ = V₂ / T₂ (Direct relationship with Kelvin T)
Gay-Lussac's Law (constant V, n)P₁ / T₁ = P₂ / T₂ (Direct relationship with Kelvin T)
Combined Gas Law (constant n)(P₁ × V₁) / T₁ = (P₂ × V₂) / T₂
Ideal Gas Equation & Gas Mixtures
Ideal Gas LawP × V = n × R × T
Universal Gas Constant (R)0.08206 L · atm / (mol · K) = 8.314 J / (mol · K)
Gas Density & Molar Massd = (P × M) / (R × T)  |  M = (m × R × T) / (P × V)
Dalton's Partial PressuresP_total = P_A + P_B + ...  |  P_A = X_A × P_total
Collecting Gas Over WaterP_dry = P_total - P_water vapor (at measured water temperature)
Storyline Anchor: Hyperbaric Physiology & Scuba Diving
Hydrostatic Depth PressureEvery 10 meters (~33 ft) of seawater adds 1.0 atm of hydrostatic pressure (P_ambient = 1 atm + depth_m / 10).
